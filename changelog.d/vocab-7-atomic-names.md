kind: **BREAKING**: vulkan vocabulary version 7, `a64` `af32` `af64` (kiss-vulkan-vocab 0.6.0)
---
Adds the atomic capability names `a64`, `af32` and `af64` to the `vulkan:` `<arith>` field (KISS#837, KISS 0.19.0): `vulkane::safe::PhysicalDevice::shader_atomic_features` / `ShaderAtomicFeatures` read `VkPhysicalDeviceShaderAtomicInt64Features` and `VK_EXT_shader_atomic_float` (chained only when the device lists the extension; a failed extension enumeration declines), and `vulkane::kiss::DeviceCapabilities` derives the names from them. Breaking for tokens: a device with 64-bit atomics now spells `a64`. `kiss-vulkan-vocab` moves to 0.6.0 on its own version line (CHANGELOG section above, with the migration); the manifest changes in three lines. Verified: `cargo test -p kiss-vulkan-vocab` (committed manifest byte-identical to a fresh emission), `kiss_target_live` 16/16 and `--lib kiss` 8/8 under gpu-run with `--features kiss-target`, `cargo fmt --all -- --check`. ROADMAP row 8d-cap.
